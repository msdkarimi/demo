# Labels — taxonomy v2

Label each row by the **first user message**: what did the user want done?

- `label`: exactly one of the names below, copied verbatim.
- `None of these`: the message fits no category (too vague, empty, or a topic the taxonomy lacks).
- `label_2`: optional, only if the message clearly asks for two things.
- `notes`: anything borderline — write down the rule you applied, and reuse it.
- Don't look at `predictions.csv` until you've finished.

## Categories

- **Coding & software development** — Writing, debugging, explaining or reviewing code in any language, including SQL, spreadsheet formulas and scripts.
- **IT & tech support** — Questions about hardware, software, networks, devices and how to set up or troubleshoot them, without writing code.
- **Business, finance & strategy** — Business plans, financial statements, pricing, markets, economics and strategic decisions.
- **Business communication** — Drafting or polishing emails, formal letters and short workplace messages.
- **Marketing, SEO & product copy** — Copy meant to sell or promote: product listings, SEO titles and keywords, ads, social posts.
- **Long-form writing & editing** — Non-fiction long-form text: essays, articles, blog posts, e-book chapters, and rewriting or proofreading text.
- **Translation & languages** — Translating text between languages and questions about language use.
- **Careers & HR** — CVs, cover letters, job applications, interviews, performance reviews and people management.
- **Science, health & learning** — Factual questions and study help in science, medicine, biology, chemistry and other academic subjects.
- **Image generation prompts** — Writing prompts for image generators (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion) and visual style descriptions.
- **Creative writing & role-play** — Fiction of any kind: stories, scripts, fan fiction, parodies, crossovers, invented worlds and interactive role-play.
- **Jailbreak & persona prompts** — Attempts to bypass the assistant's rules (DAN, Developer Mode) and 'act as' persona setups, plus questions about the model itself.
- **Chit-chat & other** — Greetings, small talk, jokes, riddles, tests and requests too short or unclear to classify.
- **None of these** — fits none of the above.
